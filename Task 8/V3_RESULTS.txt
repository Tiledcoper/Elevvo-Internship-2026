Why performance is limited
---------------------------
The main limitation is the dataset structure rather than the inference hardware.
The project uses 43 traffic-sign classes, but the available dataset contains only
900 images in total (480 train / 120 validation / 300 test). Several classes have
very few labeled examples, especially in the validation split, which makes it
hard for the model to learn and generalize equally well across all classes.
Traffic signs are also often very small inside full-resolution road scenes and
appear with complex backgrounds, making detection more difficult.

Improving the model further would benefit from a substantially larger and more
balanced labeled dataset and additional training experiments. Those experiments
would also require significantly more GPU time/resources; a stronger GPU/server
would make higher-resolution and longer training experiments more practical,
but additional data is the primary improvement needed for generalization.

Important: the reported metrics are validation metrics. The 39.84 FPS figure is
a fixed-image inference benchmark on the 300-image test set and is not a measured
webcam FPS.
